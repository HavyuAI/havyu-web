Most fitness apps are one of two things: a **notebook** that records your sets, or a **generator** that hands you a workout. Neither tells you the thing that actually builds muscle: **when you're ready to lift more.** Havyu is built entirely around that moment. This review covers what it does, what it costs, and the choices it makes on purpose.

## What is Havyu?

Havyu is an **AI weightlifting and recovery coach** for iPhone and Android. It has two pillars:

- **Training:** fast live logging, supersets, routines, a multi-week scheduler, voice logging and an exercise library with demonstrations and muscle maps.
- **Recovery:** the daily **“Have you…?”** check-in for sleep, protein, water, steps, mood and more, with Perfect Day streaks and weekly and monthly views.

And one coach that reads both, because progressive overload only works when recovery keeps up with training.

## What's best about Havyu?

### Live overload coaching

This is the feature that sets Havyu apart. In the middle of a session, each lift gets a clear call: **increase**, **maintain** or **ease back**, from how that lift has been moving and how recovered you are. Coming back from a layoff, it tells you to start lighter and build back over a few sessions. It's the decision a good coach makes at your shoulder, made from your own numbers.

### Ask the coach

Tap a question (*How is my strength trending? Am I ready for today's session? Is my sleep helping or hurting my training?*) and get an answer drawn from your own training and recovery. No blank chat box to fill, and no generic advice.

### Today's focus and coaching insights

Before you train, Today's focus tells you what has stalled and how last night's sleep should shape the session. Coaching insights surface the patterns across weeks: which lifts are climbing, where your estimated one-rep max is heading, and when a lighter week is paying off.

### The “Have you…?” check-in

Recovery tracking that people actually keep doing. One question per card, answered with a tap; the card flies away, and a full day takes under a minute. Answering honestly counts, so a logged miss is still useful data. Steps and body weight fill themselves in from Apple Health or Health Connect.

### Grounded in science

Havyu checks your targets against the research and names the study. Set a protein goal and it shows you where that sits against the evidence on muscle growth, so you know why the number matters.

### It's free to start, and private

The free tier is the full product at lower resolution: unlimited logging, the check-in, charts, history, scheduling and an on-device coach, with no time limit. Your data is stored on your phone; AI coaching is opt-in and never used to train models.[^1]

## What does Havyu deliberately not do?

**It doesn't hand you a generic programme.** Workout generators give two people with the same goal the same plan. Havyu's coach works from your training, so you build routines yourself or with the AI Routine Builder, and the coach tells you how they're working and when to push.

**It doesn't make you wear a device to bed.** Recovery comes from a one-minute check-in, plus steps and body weight from your health app. You get a recovery-aware coach without a wrist sensor.

**It doesn't lock you into one platform.** Havyu runs on iPhone and Android, and a backup moves your full history between them.

## Who is Havyu for?

- **Lifters chasing progressive overload** who want to know when to add weight, not just record that they did.
- **Anyone stuck on a plateau** who suspects sleep, food or consistency is the cause, and wants to see it in their own numbers.
- **Android users** who've been left out of AI lifting coaches.
- **Beginners** who want structure and clear calls, and **experienced lifters** fighting for the next kilo.

## Verdict

Havyu is the most complete progressive overload coach you can download in 2026. It reads both sides of your progress, makes the call that matters (*are you ready to add weight?*) and does it on iPhone and Android, free to start. If your goal is strength and muscle, start here.
