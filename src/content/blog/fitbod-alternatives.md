Fitbod's pitch is that an algorithm picks your workout. That solves the easy problem, deciding which exercises to do, and leaves the hard one untouched: **knowing whether today is the day to push.** That depends on how you slept, what you ate and how your last few sessions moved. Fitbod doesn't track your sleep, protein or water, so it can't know.[^1]

**Havyu** is built around that question. It's an **AI weightlifting and recovery coach** that reads your training and your recovery together, and tells you when each lift is ready to go up.

## Why does recovery matter more than exercise selection?

Because muscle grows through progressive overload, and overload only works when you're recovered enough to lift more than last time. A perfectly chosen exercise on four hours of sleep still stalls. Short sleep can shift weight loss away from fat and towards muscle,[^3] and protein intake sets a ceiling on how much muscle your training can build.[^4]

Havyu's daily **“Have you…?”** check-in captures exactly those habits in under a minute: one question per card, answered with a tap. The coach then connects them to your lifts, so you see that your squat moves on weeks you sleep seven hours and stalls on weeks you don't.

## What else does Havyu do?

- **Live overload coaching:** each lift gets an increase, maintain or ease-back call mid-session.
- **Today's focus:** before you train, what has stalled and how last night should shape the session.
- **Ask the coach:** tap a question (how is my strength trending? which habit would move the needle most?) and get an answer from your own data.
- **Grounded in science:** targets are checked against the research, with the study named.
- **Your plan, your exercises.** Build routines yourself or with the AI Routine Builder; the coach then tells you how they're working.

## Is it worth switching?

If you're paying for Fitbod to decide your exercises, you're paying more than twice as much for the less important half of the problem. Havyu is free to start, $39.99 a year for the full AI layer, and it answers the question that actually decides your progress: are you ready to add weight today?
