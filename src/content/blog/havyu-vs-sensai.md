Havyu and SensAI both call themselves AI fitness coaches, but they're built for different lifters. **Havyu is an AI weightlifting and recovery coach for iPhone and Android.** It tracks your training and your daily recovery in one app, then tells you when you're ready to add weight, from your own numbers. SensAI is an iPhone-only app whose chat coach generates and rewrites a training plan for you.

If you're on Android, the decision is already made: SensAI doesn't have an Android app.[^1][^2] If you're on iPhone, the difference that matters is how the coach works, and what it costs to find out.

## What's the difference between Havyu and SensAI?

| | Havyu | SensAI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platforms | iPhone and Android | iPhone only (iOS 26+) |
| Free tier | Yes, with an on-device coach | No (7-day trial) |
| Price per year | $39.99 | $69.99 |
| Price per month | $4.99 (14-day free trial) | $6.99 |
| How the coach works | Reads your own training and recovery, tells you when to add weight | Chat coach generates and rewrites your plan |
| Recovery tracking | One-tap “Have you…?” check-in: sleep, protein, water, steps and more | Wearable data via Apple Health |
| Health platforms | Apple Health and Health Connect | Apple Health |

Havyu's figures are from its [service description](/service). SensAI's are from its App Store listing.[^1]

## How does Havyu's coach work?

Havyu's coach watches both sides of your progress, the training and the recovery, and connects them. During a session it gives you a clear call on each lift: **increase**, **maintain** or **ease back**, based on how that lift has been moving and how recovered you are. Before a session, Today's focus tells you what has stalled and how last night should shape today.

The point is that the advice comes from your data, not a template. Havyu won't tell you to do a programme somebody else would get with the same goal. It tells you that your bench press is ready to go up because your last three sessions moved well and you slept and ate properly before them.

The recovery side is built for speed. The daily **“Have you…?”** check-in asks one question per card: have you slept seven hours, hit your protein, drunk enough water? You answer, the card flies away, and a full check-in takes under a minute. That's what feeds the coach: honest recovery data, every day, without a food diary or a wearable on your wrist.

## How does SensAI's coach work?

SensAI is a conversational coach built on a large language model. You describe your goals, equipment and schedule, and it generates a plan and adjusts it week to week, drawing on heart-rate variability, sleep and resting heart rate from Apple Health.[^1] It's an iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple Health product throughout.

## Is Havyu cheaper than SensAI?

Yes, on every plan:

- **Havyu Pro** is **$4.99 a month** (with a 14-day free trial) or **$39.99 a year**.
- **SensAI Premium** is **$6.99 a month** or **$69.99 a year**.[^1]

Over a year, SensAI costs **75% more**. The bigger gap is the free tier. Havyu's free version is the full product at lower resolution: logging, the “Have you…?” check-in, charts, history, scheduling and an on-device coach all cost nothing. SensAI has a 7-day trial and nothing after it.[^1]

## Which one works on Android?

Only Havyu. SensAI's App Store listing requires iOS 26 or later, and SensAI's own review of its app confirms there's no Android version.[^1][^2] Havyu is on Google Play with the same coach, the same check-in and Health Connect support.

That also matters if you switch phones. Havyu keeps your data on your device and exports a full backup, so moving between iPhone and Android doesn't mean starting again.

## What about privacy?

Havyu is local-first: your workouts and habits are stored on your phone, not in a cloud account. AI coaching is opt-in, the consent screen lists exactly what's sent, your name and email are never sent with it, and your data is never used to train AI models.[^3]

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Havyu if** you want a coach that tells you when to add weight from your own lifts and recovery, you want to try it free with no time limit, you're on Android, or you'd rather pay $39.99 a year than $69.99.

**SensAI suits** iPhone users who want a chat coach to write their week for them, and who are happy to pay from day eight.

For most lifters, the answer is Havyu: it's on both platforms, it's free to start, and it's built around the one question that drives progress: are you ready to add weight today?
