Switching apps usually means starting again: no history, no personal records, no idea what you lifted last month. Havyu is built so you don't have to. **Bring your whole history with you, and your coach starts working from it on day one.**

## Which apps can I import from?

- **Strong:** both export formats, older and current.
- **Hevy:** the workout export.
- **FitNotes:** on iPhone and Android.
- **Any spreadsheet saved as CSV:** Havyu suggests which column is which, and you confirm.
- **A Havyu backup:** for moving to a new phone, including between iPhone and Android.

## Step 1: export your data

**From Hevy:** go to Profile, Settings, Export & Import Data, Export Data, and choose to export your workouts.[^1] Keep the app in English for the export.

**From Strong:** use the data export option in Strong's settings to save a CSV file.

**From FitNotes:** use its export or backup option to save a CSV.

Save the file somewhere your phone can reach it, such as Files or Google Drive.

## Step 2: import it into Havyu

1. Open **Havyu**, go to **Settings**, then **Import Data**.
2. Choose your file. Havyu reads it **on your phone**; nothing is uploaded.[^2]
3. Answer only what Havyu needs to know. If your file doesn't say whether it's in kg or lb, Havyu asks, and shows your heaviest set so the answer is obvious. If dates could be read two ways, it asks which.
4. Check your exercises. Havyu matches each one to its library. Anything it isn't sure about is shown with suggestions for you to confirm; nothing is matched silently.
5. Review the preview, then tap import.

Large histories import in chunks, so the app stays responsive even with years of sets.

## What gets imported, and what doesn't?

- **Every working set** you logged, with its date, weight, reps and effort rating where the file has them.
- **Warm-up sets are left out**, so they don't drag your numbers down.
- **Cardio and timed rows are left out**, because they have no reps to coach from.
- **Assisted movements import at zero load**, so more assistance never reads as “stronger”.
- **Duplicates are skipped.** Import the same file twice and Havyu tells you there's nothing new.

The preview counts everything left out, so you always know what happened.

## Can I undo it?

Yes. Every import is listed under **Recent imports** on the Import Data screen, and you can undo it in one tap. Havyu also saves a safety copy of your data before every import.

## What happens next?

Your coach now has your full history. Open the Training tab and you'll see your progress, your North Star lift and coaching insights drawn from years of sets, not one session. Start your next workout and each lift gets a live call (**increase**, **maintain** or **ease back**) from how it's been moving.

Then add the other half of the picture: do your first [“Have you…?” check-in](/blog/have-you-check-in), and connect Apple Health or Health Connect to fill in steps and body weight. From there, Havyu reads your training and your recovery together, which is something neither Strong nor Hevy can do.
