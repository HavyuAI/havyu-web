Strong is a good notebook. It records your sets cleanly and stays out of the way. But a notebook can't tell you anything you don't already know: it won't spot that your bench has stalled for three weeks, it won't notice that the stall started when your sleep dropped, and it won't tell you when you're ready to add weight again.

That's the gap **Havyu** fills. It's an **AI weightlifting and recovery coach**: everything you liked about logging in Strong, with a coach on top that's built around progressive overload.

## What does Havyu do that Strong can't?

**It coaches progressive overload.** In the middle of a session, each lift gets a clear call: **increase**, **maintain** or **ease back**, based on how that lift has been moving and how recovered you are. Coming back after time off, it tells you to start lighter and build back, instead of letting you max out on day one.

**It reads your recovery.** Strong only knows what happens in the gym. Havyu's daily **“Have you…?”** check-in records sleep, protein, water, steps and more in under a minute, and the coach connects them to your lifts. That's how it can tell you a stall lines up with a run of short nights.

**It explains your progress.** Coaching insights, Today's focus and Ask the coach turn your history into plain-language answers: what's trending up, what's stalled, and what to watch today.

## Will I lose my Strong history?

No. Havyu imports **both** Strong export formats, old and new. It reads your units from the file where Strong includes them and asks you when it doesn't, so a 100 kg bench never turns into 100 lb. Warm-ups are left out so they don't drag your numbers down, and every exercise is matched to Havyu's library, with any uncertain match shown to you rather than guessed. The import runs on your phone and can be undone.[^2]

Your coach then starts with your full history on day one, so the first increase call is based on years of sets, not one week. The step-by-step is in [how to switch from Strong or Hevy](/blog/switch-from-strong-or-hevy).

## What does it cost?

Havyu's free tier has unlimited logging, the check-in, charts, history, scheduling and an on-device coach, with no time limit. Havyu Pro adds the AI coaching layer for **$4.99 a month** (14-day free trial) or **$39.99 a year**.

If you want a notebook, Strong is a notebook. If you want to keep adding weight to the bar, you need something that reads your training and your recovery and tells you when the next jump is due. That's Havyu.
